MedRecall is a personal health record app for iOS. Connect your own Medicare claims (via CMS's Blue Button 2.0 API), hospital patient portal records (via Epic on FHIR), and Apple Health data to see everything in one place.

Every record stays on your device — MedRecall does not operate a backend server, and nothing you connect is sold or shared with third parties for marketing.

What MedRecall does

  • Pulls in your Medicare Part A/B/D claims history directly from Medicare, with your authorization.
  • Connects to participating hospital patient portals to import visit notes, conditions, and medications.
  • Brings in health metrics you choose to share from Apple Health.
  • Records and summarizes doctor visits, entirely on-device, using Apple's on-device AI (Apple Intelligence) — no transcript ever leaves your phone.
  • Builds shareable PDF reports — visit history, medications, a health summary for a new doctor, and more.
