This Privacy Policy explains how MedRecall (“the App,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the App. MedRecall is a personal health record app that lets you gather your own medical information — including data from Medicare, hospital patient portals, and Apple Health — into one place on your own device.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information only when you choose to connect a data source or enter it yourself:

  • Medicare data (via CMS's Blue Button 2.0 API): When you connect your Medicare account, and after you authorize the connection through Medicare's own OAuth 2.0 sign-in, we retrieve your Medicare enrollment/coverage information and claims (Explanation of Benefit) records using the Blue Button 2.0 API, the official CMS data-sharing API for Medicare beneficiaries.
  • Hospital / patient portal data (via Epic FHIR): With your authorization through a similar OAuth 2.0 sign-in, we retrieve records such as visit notes, conditions, and medications from hospital systems that support the Epic on FHIR platform.
  • Apple Health data: With your permission, we read health metrics you choose to share (such as blood glucose, blood oxygen, heart rate variability, ECG, resting heart rate, respiratory rate, falls, steps, sleep, and body measurements) and write back blood pressure and weight entries you log in the App.
  • Calendar and Reminders: With your permission, we create and read calendar events for appointments you schedule in the App, and can create reminders you request.
  • Google Sign-In and Google Drive: If you choose to back up your data, we use Google Sign-In and request access limited to backup files the App itself creates in your Google Drive.
  • Information you enter directly: doctors, medications, appointments, visit notes, and voice recordings (and their transcripts) you create in the App.

We do not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or any third-party tracking technology.

2. How We Use Your Information, Including On-Device AI

We use the information above solely to operate the App for you: to display your combined medical history, remind you of appointments and medications, and back up your data at your request. We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell, rent, or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

Some features — summarizing a recorded visit into a short conclusion and action items, answering questions you ask about your own saved visit history, and explaining a medical term in plain language — use Apple's on-device Foundation Models (Apple Intelligence). This processing happens entirely on your iPhone; your transcripts and records are not sent to us, to Apple, or to any other server or AI provider to generate these results.

3. Your Consent

Each data source above is opt-in and independent: connecting Medicare, a hospital portal, Google, or Apple Health is always a separate choice you make for that source alone, and declining or skipping one does not prevent you from using the rest of the App. We only request the specific Medicare or hospital-portal data needed for the features described above, and only after you complete that source's own sign-in and authorization screen.

4. How We Share Information

We share information only as necessary to provide the features you use, and only at your direction:

  • With CMS's Blue Button 2.0 API and hospital FHIR endpoints, solely to retrieve your own records after you authorize that specific connection.
  • With Apple (Health, Calendar, Reminders) and Google (Sign-In, Drive), governed by the device and account permissions you grant.

Other than as described above, we do not disclose your information to any third party, we never sell your data, and no de-identified, anonymized, or pseudonymized version of your data is shared with anyone either.

5. Where Your Information Is Stored

MedRecall does not operate a backend server that stores your health data. Your records are stored locally on your device. Access tokens and other credentials are stored in your device's Keychain, never in plain text. If you turn on backups, that data is stored in the Google Drive account you connect, under your control. The App can also be locked with Face ID or your device passcode.

6. Your Choices, Control, and Withdrawing Consent

You are always in control of the data connected to the App, and can withdraw your consent for any source at any time:

  • Connect or disconnect your Medicare, hospital portal, Google, or Health data sources at any time from within the App — this immediately stops any further syncing from that source.
  • Revoke the App's access to your Medicare data at any time through your Medicare.gov account, and revoke hospital-portal or Google access through those providers' own account settings — the same effect as disconnecting from within the App.
  • Delete data stored in the App at any time. Uninstalling the App removes all data stored locally on your device; any backups you created remain in your own Google Drive until you delete them there.
  • Contact us (below) with any question about your data or to request assistance deleting it.

Withdrawing consent or disconnecting a source only affects new data going forward — it does not automatically delete what was already imported before you disconnected; use the delete options above for that.

7. Data Retention, and Inactive Use

We retain nothing on any server we operate, because we don't operate one for your health data — it lives on your device and, optionally, in your own Google Drive. Disconnecting a data source or deleting the App removes the corresponding data from the App.

MedRecall does not use a server-side account, so there is no separate "account" that can go dormant or need closing — your data simply stays on your device, unsynced, for as long as the App remains installed, whether or not you open it regularly.

8. Impact on Family Members or Other Individuals

MedRecall does not collect genetic data, and does not ask you for family medical history. If a record you import (for example, from Medicare or a hospital portal) happens to reference a family member — such as a noted family history of a condition — that reference is used only to display your own record to you; it is never extracted, shared, or used to identify or make decisions about anyone else.

9. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, including HTTPS/TLS-only network connections, OAuth 2.0 with PKCE for every third-party sign-in, storage of credentials in the iOS Keychain, and an optional biometric app lock. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so while we work to protect your information we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

MedRecall is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 18.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this document reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. If we are ever acquired or MedRecall's ownership otherwise changes, we will notify users in the App and update this Privacy Policy before any change in how your data is handled takes effect.
